# Data and third-party material

Original licenses and terms apply to all third-party material:

- DL passages and judgments: [MS MARCO](https://microsoft.github.io/msmarco/)
  and [NIST TREC](https://trec.nist.gov/).
- COVID documents and judgments: [CORD-19](https://github.com/allenai/cord19)
  and [TREC-COVID](https://ir.nist.gov/covidSubmit/). Article-level terms can differ.
- Qwen and MiniLM weights: their model-card terms. Weights are downloaded separately.
- LARA and Oosterhuis reference code: the authors' licenses and notices.
  Pinned revisions are recorded in the configurations and evidence receipts.

Released numerical tables retain their source provenance. Including them does
not change the underlying benchmark rights. No new blanket code or data license
has been selected; citation metadata is not a license grant.
